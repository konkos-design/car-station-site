Which ticket do you need?

Not sure what Service NSW is asking for? Here's the short version. Tap a ticket, then give us a call — we'll tell you exactly what your car needs.

Pink slip

eSafety check

Needed
Every year to renew rego
Valid for
6 months

Who needs a pink slip?

  • Most light vehicles more than 5 years old need a yearly safety check before registration can be renewed.
  • The 5 years are counted from the date on the vehicle's compliance plate.
  • Something small not passing — a worn tyre, a blown globe, wipers? Our customers say we sort it on the spot so you can get the slip done.
Book a pink slip

Rules are set by Transport for NSW and can change — see nsw.gov.au for the official details.

Ticket · NSWAUVIS

Blue slip

Unregistered vehicle inspection

Needed
To register an unregistered car
Valid for
42 days

When do you need a blue slip?

  • Registration expired more than 3 months ago.
  • The car came from interstate or overseas.
  • The vehicle has no number plates.
  • A write-off that's been approved for re-registration.
